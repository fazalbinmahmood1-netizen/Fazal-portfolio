## Most useful SEO charts (requires actual data)
1. Weekly Search Console impressions and clicks, split into name/brand queries and non-brand service queries.
2. Clicks and click-through rate by landing page, compared with the previous period.
3. Indexed preferred URLs and exclusion reasons; check the canonical selected by Google.
4. Qualified enquiries by landing page/source, using consent-appropriate analytics or enquiry records.

Average position is diagnostic, not a business outcome. PageSpeed 100 is not proof of rankings. Do not fabricate an SEO chart without data.
